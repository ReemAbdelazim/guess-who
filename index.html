      How to play

      1. One player clicks Start a new game and sends the link (or the code) to the other player.
      2. Each player chooses Player 1 or Player 2 and types their name. Each player now has their own link.
      3. Each player secretly picks a person from the board and locks them in. Click the i on a card to learn about someone.
      4. When both are locked in, press Start clock. You have 5 minutes in total. Only the host can pause it or add time.
      5. Take turns asking one yes-or-no question. Talk it over and ask out loud, then press Done to pass the turn. You can also type the question instead, and the other player answers Yes or No on their laptop. There’s no limit on questions.
      6. Click a card to rule that person out. Click it again to bring them back.
      7. When you’re sure, or when time runs out, lock in your guess.
      8. Once both players have guessed, both secret people are revealed. If you both guessed right, you both win.
