      How to play

      1. Each team plays on its own laptop with this game open.
      2. Each team secretly chooses one person from the board and locks them in. That's who the other team has to guess.
      3. Don't know someone? Click the i on their card for a short bio and a few facts.
      4. Start the timer. Teams take turns asking the other team one yes-or-no question at a time. There's no limit on how many questions you ask.
      5. Click a card to rule that person out. Click it again to bring them back.
      6. Each team gets 5 minutes in total to ask questions.
      7. When you're sure, or when time runs out, lock in your guess.
      8. Both teams reveal their secret person. If both teams guess correctly, both teams win.
